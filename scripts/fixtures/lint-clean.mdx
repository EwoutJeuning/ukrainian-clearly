English prose with a marked word: <Uk>мо́ва</Uk>, an apostrophe in <Uk>м’я́со</Uk> and <Uk>п’ять</Uk>, and ordinary English quotes: it's "fine", 'quoted', café.

Transliteration may carry a stress mark on Latin letters: móva.
