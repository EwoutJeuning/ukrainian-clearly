term: Transliteration
short: "Writing Ukrainian words in Latin letters, letter by letter, using a fixed table."
ukTerm: транслітера́ція

---

Ukraine has an official transliteration system, approved by the Cabinet of Ministers in 2010. It sets the Latin spelling of names and places, as in <span lang="uk">Ки́їв</span> → Kyiv.

Transliteration is not a pronunciation guide. It does not show stress, it drops the soft sign and the apostrophe, and some Latin letters stand for sounds that are not the English ones.
