---
term: Preposition
short: "A short word such as “in”, “on” or “without” that comes before a noun and needs that noun in a particular case."
ukTerm: прийме́нник
related: [grammar/prepositions, grammar/case-overview]
seeAlso: [grammatical-case, genitive, instrumental, locative]
---

In Ukrainian, the noun after a preposition is always in a particular case, and usually its ending changes: <span lang="uk">без цу́кру</span> (without sugar, genitive), <span lang="uk">з молоко́м</span> (with milk, instrumental).

Some prepositions take one case, others two with a difference in meaning. Learn each preposition in a short phrase together with its case.
