---
term: Dative
short: "The case for the person something is given or said to, or done for."
ukTerm: дава́льний відмі́нок
related: [grammar/case-overview]
seeAlso: [grammatical-case]
---

The dative usually answers “to whom?” or “for whom?”. Some verbs are followed by the dative where English uses a direct object: <span lang="uk">Я телефону́ю сестрі́.</span> (I'm phoning my sister.)
