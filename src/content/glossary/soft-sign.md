---
term: Soft sign
short: "The letter Ь, which has no sound of its own and shows that the consonant before it is soft."
ukTerm: м’яки́й знак
related: [alphabet, alphabet/pronunciation]

---

The soft sign is the letter <span lang="uk">Ь</span>. It is not pronounced by itself. It shows that the consonant before it is soft (palatalised), roughly as if a hint of the y in *yes* were blended into it: <span lang="uk">день</span> (day).

In the official transliteration the soft sign is simply left out.
