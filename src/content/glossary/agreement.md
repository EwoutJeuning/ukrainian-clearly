---
term: Agreement
short: "A word changing its ending to match the gender, number and case of the noun it goes with."
ukTerm: узго́дження
related: [grammar/adjectives-and-agreement, grammar/nouns-and-gender, grammar/past-tense]
seeAlso: [grammatical-gender, grammatical-case]
---

Adjectives, possessives such as *my*, and past-tense verbs agree with their noun. In the singular they match its gender, and in the plural there is one form for all genders: <span lang="uk">нови́й буди́нок</span> (a new house), <span lang="uk">нова́ кни́га</span> (a new book), <span lang="uk">нове́ вікно́</span> (a new window).

Agreement follows the noun's grammatical gender, not its meaning.
