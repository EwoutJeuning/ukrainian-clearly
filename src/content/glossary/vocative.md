---
term: Vocative
short: "The form used when you speak to someone directly and call them by name or title."
ukTerm: кли́чний відмі́нок
related: [grammar/case-overview]
seeAlso: [grammatical-case]
---

When you address someone, their name or title takes the vocative ending: <span lang="uk">Ма́мо, дя́кую!</span> (Thank you, Mum!) The stress can move in the vocative, so learn it with the word.
