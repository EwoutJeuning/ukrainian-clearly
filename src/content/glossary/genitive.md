---
term: Genitive
short: "The case that often means “of”, and is used after “there is no” and after some prepositions."
ukTerm: родовий відмі́нок
related: [grammar/case-overview, grammar/negation, grammar/prepositions]
seeAlso: [grammatical-case, preposition]
---

The genitive often shows what something belongs to or is part of, like English *of* or *'s*: <span lang="uk">Це кни́га сестри́.</span> (This is my sister's book.)

It is also used after a negative such as “there is no…”, and after some prepositions, such as <span lang="uk">без</span> (without).
