---
term: Apostrophe
short: "A mark inside a word showing that a hard consonant is said separately from the я, ю, є or ї after it."
ukTerm: апостроф
related: [alphabet, alphabet/pronunciation]
seeAlso: [soft-sign]
---

The apostrophe is not a letter of the alphabet, but it changes the pronunciation. It is written between a hard consonant and <span lang="uk">я, ю, є</span> or <span lang="uk">ї</span>, and it tells you to pronounce the <span lang="uk">й</span> sound at the start of that vowel: <span lang="uk">м’я́со</span> (meat).

This site writes the apostrophe as ’, as the official orthography does. In the official transliteration it is left out.
