---
term: Inflection
short: "Changing the ending of a word to show its grammatical job, such as its case, number, person or tense."
ukTerm: словозмі́на
related: [grammar, grammar/case-overview]
seeAlso: [grammatical-case, conjugation, agreement]
---

In Ukrainian, nouns, adjectives, pronouns and verbs all change their endings. A noun changes for case and number: <span lang="uk">кни́га</span> (a book, as the subject) but <span lang="uk">Я чита́ю кни́гу.</span> (I'm reading a book.)

Changing the endings of nouns, adjectives and pronouns is also called declension. Changing the endings of verbs is called conjugation.
