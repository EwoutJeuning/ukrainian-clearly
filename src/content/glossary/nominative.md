term: Nominative
short: "The case of the subject, and the dictionary form of a noun."

related: [grammar/case-overview]
seeAlso: [grammatical-case, accusative]
---

The nominative is the form you find in a dictionary. It is used for the subject of the sentence, the one doing the action, and after *this is*: <span lang="uk">Це моя́ сестра́.</span> (This is my sister.)
