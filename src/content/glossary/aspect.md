---
term: Aspect
short: "The difference between an imperfective verb (a process or a repeated action) and a perfective verb (a completed action with a result)."
ukTerm: вид дієсло́ва
related: [grammar/aspect, grammar/past-tense, grammar/future-tense]
seeAlso: [infinitive, conjugation]
---

Most Ukrainian verbs come in pairs that share one English translation. The imperfective verb (<span lang="uk">недоко́наний вид</span>) presents the action as a process, or as something repeated. The perfective verb (<span lang="uk">доко́наний вид</span>) presents it as completed, with a result.

The perfective is often the imperfective with a prefix: <span lang="uk">чита́ти</span> and <span lang="uk">прочита́ти</span> both mean “to read”. Perfective verbs have no present tense: their present-looking forms refer to the future.
