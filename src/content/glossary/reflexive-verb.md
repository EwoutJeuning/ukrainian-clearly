---
term: Reflexive verb
short: "A verb ending in -ся (or -сь), which stays at the very end of every form."
ukTerm: зворо́тне дієсло́во
related: [grammar/present-tense, grammar/past-tense]
seeAlso: [conjugation, infinitive]
---

Reflexive verbs are conjugated like other verbs, with <span lang="uk">-ся</span> added after the ending: <span lang="uk">навча́тися</span> (to study) → <span lang="uk">я навча́юся</span> (I study).

The name comes from verbs like *to wash oneself*, but many reflexive verbs have no “oneself” meaning in English. After a vowel, dictionaries also give a shorter <span lang="uk">-сь</span>.
