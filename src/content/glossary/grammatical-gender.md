---
term: Grammatical gender
short: "The class a noun belongs to: masculine, feminine or neuter."
ukTerm: рід
related: [grammar/nouns-and-gender, grammar/adjectives-and-agreement]
seeAlso: [agreement, inflection]
---

Every Ukrainian noun in the singular is masculine, feminine or neuter. This is a grammatical category, not a statement about sex: things have a gender too. <span lang="uk">Стіл</span> (table) is masculine, <span lang="uk">кни́га</span> (book) is feminine and <span lang="uk">вікно́</span> (window) is neuter.

Gender matters because adjectives, past-tense verbs and pronouns change their endings to match it. The ending of the dictionary form is a good guide to the gender, but not a perfect one. A dictionary gives the gender of every noun.
