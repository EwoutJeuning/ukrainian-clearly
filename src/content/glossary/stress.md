---
term: Stress
short: "The syllable of a word that stands out from the others; in Ukrainian, mainly by being longer."
ukTerm: на́голос
related: [alphabet/stress-and-transliteration, alphabet/pronunciation]
seeAlso: [transliteration, inflection]
---

A Ukrainian word of more than one syllable normally has one stressed syllable (a few words allow two pronunciations). The stress can fall on any syllable, and it can move to a different syllable in another form of the same word, so it has to be learned with each word.

Ordinary Ukrainian text does not show the stress. This site marks it in examples with an accent over the stressed vowel. For example, the stress moves in the plural of the word for water: <span lang="uk">вода́</span> (water), <span lang="uk">во́ди</span> (waters).
