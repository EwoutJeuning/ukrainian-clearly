---
term: Accusative
short: "The case of the direct object: the person or thing the action is done to."
ukTerm: знахі́дний відмі́нок
related: [grammar/case-overview, grammar/prepositions]
seeAlso: [grammatical-case, nominative]
---

The accusative marks the direct object: <span lang="uk">Я ба́чу сестру́.</span> (I see my sister.) It is also used after some prepositions, for example for direction after <span lang="uk">в</span> or <span lang="uk">на</span>.

For some nouns the accusative looks the same as the nominative, so you cannot always see it from the ending.
