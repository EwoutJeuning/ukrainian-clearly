---
term: Instrumental
short: "The case for the tool or means, and for “with” after the preposition з."
ukTerm: ору́дний відмі́нок
related: [grammar/case-overview, grammar/prepositions]
seeAlso: [grammatical-case, preposition]
---

The instrumental shows what you do something with or by means of. The ending alone can do the job of English *with*: <span lang="uk">Я пишу́ ру́чкою.</span> (I write with a pen.)

After the preposition <span lang="uk">з</span> it shows who or what you are together with.
