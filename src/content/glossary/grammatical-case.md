---
term: Case
short: "The form a noun, pronoun or adjective takes to show its job in the sentence."
ukTerm: відмі́нок
related: [grammar/case-overview, grammar/prepositions]
seeAlso: [nominative, genitive, dative, accusative, instrumental, locative, vocative, inflection]
---

Ukrainian shows who does what mainly with the endings of words, not with word order. Each set of endings for one job is a case. Ukrainian has seven: nominative, genitive, dative, accusative, instrumental, locative and vocative.

English has a small remnant of this in *he* and *him*. Ukrainian does it with every noun: <span lang="uk">сестра́</span> (sister, as the subject) but <span lang="uk">сестру́</span> (sister, as the direct object).
