---
term: Locative
short: "The case used only after a preposition, often to say where someone or something is."
ukTerm: місце́вий відмі́нок
related: [grammar/case-overview, grammar/prepositions]
seeAlso: [grammatical-case, preposition]
---

The locative never appears without a preposition. Its most common use is for place, after <span lang="uk">в</span> or <span lang="uk">у</span> (in) and <span lang="uk">на</span> (on, at): <span lang="uk">Я живу́ в Ки́єві.</span> (I live in Kyiv.)

It is also called the prepositional case.
