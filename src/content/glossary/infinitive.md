---
term: Infinitive
short: "The dictionary form of a verb, ending in -ти, like English “to …”."
ukTerm: інфініти́в
related: [grammar/present-tense, grammar/past-tense, grammar/future-tense]
seeAlso: [conjugation, aspect]
---

Ukrainian dictionaries list verbs in the infinitive, which ends in <span lang="uk">-ти</span>: <span lang="uk">чита́ти</span> (to read). It does not change for person or tense.

You build the past tense from the infinitive, and one way of making the future uses the infinitive after a form of “to be”.
