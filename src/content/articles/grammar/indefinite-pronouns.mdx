After this page you will be able to say *someone*, *something*, *somewhere* and *some day*, and *anyone*, *anything*, *anywhere*. You will know the difference between <Uk>хтось</Uk> (someone) and <Uk>будь-хто́</Uk> (anyone at all), how these words change their form (<Uk>хтось</Uk> → <Uk>кого́сь</Uk>), and how to spell them: together, with a hyphen, or as separate words.

## Built from question words

Ukrainian makes these words from the question words you know from [Questions](/grammar/questions/): <Uk>хто</Uk>, <Uk>що</Uk>, <Uk>де</Uk>, <Uk>коли́</Uk>, <Uk>куди́</Uk>, <Uk>яки́й</Uk>. A small part added at the start or the end changes the meaning.

    ['куди́ (where to)', 'куди́сь (somewhere, to some place)', 'будь-куди, куди́-не́будь (anywhere, to any place)', 'ніку́ди (nowhere, to no place)'],
    ['яки́й (which, what kind)', 'яки́йсь (some, a certain)', 'будь-яки́й, яки́й-не́будь (any)', 'нія́кий (no, not any)'],
  ]}

/>

The words with <Uk>ні-</Uk> belong to [negation](/grammar/negation/): they go with <Uk>не</Uk> before the verb, as in <Uk>Ніхто́ не зна́є.</Uk> (Nobody knows.) This page is about the other columns.

## -сь: someone in particular

The ending <Uk>-сь</Uk> means a particular person, thing, place or time that the speaker does not know or does not name. The dictionary defines <Uk>хтось</Uk> as <Uk>«невідомо хто; той, кого не можуть або не хочуть назвати»</Uk>: "someone unknown; someone they cannot or do not want to name" [src: goroh-dictionary].

<ExampleList title="Some-">
  <Example uk="Хтось сту́кає у две́рі." en="Someone is knocking at the door." note="у before дв-." />
  <Example uk="Я щось чу́ю." en="I can hear something." />
  <Example uk="Я десь ба́чив цю жі́нку." en="I've seen this woman somewhere." note="Said by a man." />
  <Example uk="Ми коли́сь жили́ в Ки́єві." en="We once lived in Kyiv." />
  <Example uk="Він куди́сь пої́хав." en="He has gone off somewhere." />
  <Example uk="Тобі́ дзвони́ла яка́сь жі́нка." en="Some woman phoned you." note="Informal: ти. яка́сь agrees with жі́нка, like яка́." />
</ExampleList>

<Uk>Коли́сь</Uk> usually points to the past ("once, at one time"), but it can also mean "at some time" in general [src: goroh-dictionary].

## будь-, -небудь, -будь and аби-: it doesn't matter who

These parts mean that the choice does not matter. The dictionary defines <Uk>будь-хто́</Uk> as <Uk>«все одно (байдуже) хто; всякий»</Uk> (no matter who; anyone) and <Uk>хто-не́будь</Uk> as <Uk>«яка-небудь людина, байдуже хто»</Uk> (some person, no matter who) [src: goroh-dictionary]. In practice:

- **<Uk>будь-хто́</Uk>, <Uk>будь-що́</Uk>, <Uk>будь-коли</Uk>** stress the free choice: *anyone at all, anything at all, at any time you like*.
- **<Uk>хто-не́будь</Uk>, <Uk>що-не́будь</Uk>, <Uk>коли́-не́будь</Uk>** mean *someone or other, anything*: any one will do. They suit questions and requests, where the person or thing is not known yet.

<ExampleList title="Any-">
  <Example uk="Це мо́же зроби́ти будь-хто́." en="Anyone can do this." />
  <Example uk="Дзвони́ мені́ будь-коли." en="Call me any time." note="Informal: ти." />
  <Example uk="Хто-не́будь ба́чив мої́ ключі́?" en="Has anyone seen my keys?" />
  <Example uk="Ти коли́-не́будь був у Льво́ві?" en="Have you ever been to Lviv?" note="Informal: ти, said to a man." />
  <Example uk="Розкажи́ що-не́будь." en="Tell me something (anything)." note="Informal: ти." />
</ExampleList>

Compare <Uk>-сь</Uk> and <Uk>-небудь</Uk> in a question. <Uk>Хтось дзвони́в?</Uk> asks about a particular call: *Did someone call?* <Uk>Хто-не́будь дзвони́в?</Uk> asks more openly: *Did anyone call?* The difference is small, and both are correct.

### Less common forms

- **<Uk>хто-бу́дь</Uk>, <Uk>що-бу́дь</Uk>** (with <Uk>-будь</Uk> at the end) mean much the same as <Uk>хто-не́будь</Uk>: <Uk>«не має значення, байдуже хто»</Uk> (it doesn't matter who) [src: goroh-dictionary].
- **<Uk>аби́хто</Uk>, <Uk>аби́що</Uk>** also mean "anyone, anything", and the dictionary explains <Uk>аби́хто</Uk> as <Uk>«будь-хто, хто-небудь, кожний»</Uk> [src: goroh-dictionary]. One of its examples has <Uk>аби́ з ким не</Uk>: *not with just anyone*.

## Дехто and дещо: some people, a few things

<Uk>Де́хто</Uk> is not "someone" but "some people": the dictionary defines it as <Uk>«деякі люди, невелика група людей із числа інших»</Uk> (some people, a small group among others) [src: goroh-dictionary]. The verb is singular. <Uk>Де́що</Uk> means "something, a few things".

<ExampleList title="Some people, some things">
  <Example uk="Де́хто ще не прийшо́в." en="Some people haven't come yet." note="Singular verb: прийшо́в." />
  <Example uk="Де́кому це не подо́бається." en="Some people don't like this." note="Dative: де́кому." />
  <Example uk="Я хо́чу де́що сказа́ти." en="I want to say something." />
</ExampleList>

## How they change their form

Only the part that comes from <Uk>хто</Uk>, <Uk>що</Uk> or <Uk>яки́й</Uk> changes, in the same way as the question word in [all cases](/grammar/pronoun-cases/). The added part stays as it is: <Uk>-сь</Uk> at the end, <Uk>будь-</Uk> and <Uk>де-</Uk> at the start.

    ['Accusative', 'кого́сь', 'щось', 'будь-кого́'],
    ['Instrumental', 'кимось', 'чи́мось', 'будь-ким'],
    ['Locative', '(на) ко́мусь', '(на) чо́мусь', 'будь на ко́му'],
  ]}

/>

All the forms in the table are from the dictionary [src: goroh-dictionary]. The locative is always used after a preposition; with <Uk>будь-</Uk> the preposition goes in the middle (see below). Note the stress: the dative is <Uk>кому́сь</Uk>, but the locative is <Uk>ко́мусь</Uk>. The dictionary gives <Uk>кимось</Uk> without a stress mark, so it is left unmarked here. <Uk>Хто-не́будь</Uk> changes the same way: <Uk>кого́-не́будь</Uk>, <Uk>кому́-не́будь</Uk>, <Uk>ким-не́будь</Uk>.

<ExampleList title="In different cases">
  <Example uk="Ти кого́сь чека́єш?" en="Are you waiting for someone?" note="Informal: ти." />
  <Example uk="Я хо́чу щось купи́ти ма́мі." en="I want to buy something for Mum." />
  <Example uk="Вона́ розмовля́є з кимось по телефо́ну." en="She is talking to someone on the phone." note="Instrumental after з." />
  <Example uk="Запита́й кого́-не́будь." en="Ask someone (anyone)." note="Informal: ти." />
</ExampleList>

### With a preposition

When a preposition is used with <Uk>будь-</Uk>, <Uk>де-</Uk>, <Uk>аби-</Uk> or <Uk>ні-</Uk>, it usually goes **between** the two parts, and the words are then written separately: <Uk>будь у ко́го</Uk>, <Uk>будь на чо́му</Uk> [src: pravopys-2026, §39]; <Uk>ні з ким</Uk>, <Uk>ні до ко́го</Uk> [src: pravopys-2026, §44]; <Uk>де з ким</Uk>, <Uk>аби́ з ким</Uk> [src: goroh-dictionary]. With <Uk>-сь</Uk> and <Uk>-небудь</Uk> the preposition simply comes first: <Uk>з кимось</Uk>, <Uk>про що-не́будь</Uk>.

## Spelling: together, hyphen or apart

The orthography sorts these words by the part that is added [src: pravopys-2026, §39, §41, §44]:

<GrammarTable
  caption="How to write indefinite words"
  headers={['Written', 'With', 'Examples']}
  rows={[
    ['as one word', 'аби-, ані-, де-, -сь', 'аби́хто, де́хто, де́що, хтось, щось, яки́йсь, десь, коли́сь, куди́сь'],
    ['with a hyphen', 'будь-, -будь, -небудь, казна-, хтозна-, бозна-', 'будь-хто́, будь-яки́й, будь-коли, хто-бу́дь, хто-не́будь, де-не́будь, ка́зна-що́'],
    ['as separate words', 'a preposition between the parts', 'будь у ко́го, ка́зна з ким, ні з ким, ні до ко́го'],
  ]}
  ukColumns={[2]}
/>

- **Together:** the orthography lists <Uk>аби́хто</Uk>, <Uk>де́хто</Uk>, <Uk>де́що</Uk>, <Uk>хтось</Uk>, <Uk>щось</Uk>, <Uk>яки́йсь</Uk> [src: pravopys-2026, §39], and adverbs with <Uk>-сь</Uk> such as <Uk>десь</Uk>, <Uk>коли́сь</Uk>, <Uk>куди́сь</Uk>, <Uk>якось</Uk> [src: pravopys-2026, §44]. The negative <Uk>ніхто́</Uk>, <Uk>ніщо́</Uk>, <Uk>нія́кий</Uk>, <Uk>ніде́</Uk> are also one word [src: pravopys-2026, §44].
- **Hyphen:** <Uk>будь-хто́</Uk>, <Uk>будь-що́</Uk>, <Uk>хто-бу́дь</Uk>, <Uk>хто-не́будь</Uk>, <Uk>що-не́будь</Uk> [src: pravopys-2026, §39], and adverbs such as <Uk>будь-де</Uk>, <Uk>будь-коли</Uk>, <Uk>коли́-не́будь</Uk>, <Uk>куди́-не́будь</Uk> [src: pravopys-2026, §41].
- **Apart:** when a preposition stands between the parts: <Uk>будь у ко́го</Uk>, <Uk>ка́зна з ким</Uk> [src: pravopys-2026, §39], <Uk>ні в ко́го</Uk>, <Uk>ні з ким</Uk> [src: pravopys-2026, §44].

Words with <Uk>ка́зна-</Uk>, <Uk>хто́зна-</Uk> and <Uk>бо́зна-</Uk> mean roughly "who knows who, goodness knows what": <Uk>ка́зна-що́</Uk>, <Uk>бо́зна-що́</Uk> [src: goroh-dictionary].

## Anyone in a negative sentence: ні-

English uses *anyone*, *anything*, *ever* after *not*. Ukrainian uses the <Uk>ні-</Uk> words there, together with <Uk>не</Uk>. See [negation](/grammar/negation/).

<ExampleList title="Not… anyone">
  <Example uk="Я ніко́го не ба́чив." en="I didn't see anyone." note="Said by a man." />
  <Example uk="Вона́ нічо́го не сказа́ла." en="She didn't say anything." />
  <Example uk="Ми ніко́ли не були́ в Оде́сі." en="We have never been to Odesa." />
</ExampleList>

## Common mistakes and limits

<Note type="mistake">
  **Translating *not… anyone* with <Uk>будь-</Uk>.** After <Uk>не</Uk>, the usual Ukrainian for *anyone*, *anything* or *ever* is a <Uk>ні-</Uk> word: <Uk>Я ніко́го не зна́ю</Uk> (I don't know anyone). See [negation](/grammar/negation/).
</Note>

<Note type="mistake">
  **Wrong spelling.** <Uk>Хтось</Uk>, <Uk>де́хто</Uk> and <Uk>аби́хто</Uk> are one word; <Uk>будь-хто́</Uk>, <Uk>хто-не́будь</Uk> and <Uk>хто-бу́дь</Uk> have a hyphen. Not *<Uk>хто сь</Uk>*, *<Uk>будьхто</Uk>* or *<Uk>хтонебудь</Uk>* [src: pravopys-2026, §39].
</Note>

<Note type="mistake">
  **Changing the wrong part.** Only the <Uk>хто</Uk> / <Uk>що</Uk> part changes: <Uk>з кимось</Uk>, <Uk>для кого́сь</Uk>, not *<Uk>з хтось</Uk>*.
</Note>

<Note type="remember">
  <Uk>-сь</Uk>: a particular one I can't or won't name (<Uk>хтось</Uk>). <Uk>-небудь</Uk>, <Uk>будь-</Uk>: it doesn't matter which (<Uk>хто-не́будь</Uk>, <Uk>будь-хто́</Uk>). <Uk>ні-</Uk> with <Uk>не</Uk>: none at all (<Uk>ніхто́ не</Uk>).
</Note>
