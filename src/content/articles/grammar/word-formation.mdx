After this page you will be able to recognise the most common building blocks of Ukrainian words: the suffixes that make a word "small" or affectionate, the prefixes that change the meaning and aspect of a verb, the suffixes that make nouns for people, actions and qualities, and the suffixes that make feminine job titles. Knowing these parts lets you guess many new words from ones you already know.

## Diminutives: small and dear

Ukrainian uses diminutive suffixes a lot. They can mean that something is small, and they often show affection: the dictionary calls many of these words <Uk>зменшувально-пестливі</Uk> (diminutive and affectionate) [src: goroh-dictionary]. The word keeps its gender.

    ['-ечк-а', 'рі́чка (river)', 'рі́чечка', 'feminine'],
    ['-ечк-о', 'сло́во (word)', 'слове́чко', 'neuter'],
    ['-еньк-о', 'ба́тько (father)', 'ба́тенько', 'masculine'],
  ]}
  ukColumns={[1, 2]}
/>

The orthography sets the spelling of several of these suffixes [src: pravopys-2026, §32]:

- **<Uk>-ик</Uk>** is written with <Uk>и</Uk>: <Uk>бра́тик</Uk>, <Uk>ву́злик</Uk>.
- **<Uk>-ечок</Uk>, <Uk>-ечк-</Uk>** are written with <Uk>е</Uk> (<Uk>є</Uk> after a vowel): <Uk>міше́чок</Uk>, <Uk>рі́чечка</Uk>, <Uk>слове́чко</Uk>, <Uk>яє́чко</Uk>.
- **<Uk>-ок</Uk>** loses its <Uk>о</Uk> in the other cases, and after a soft consonant it is written <Uk>-ьок</Uk>: <Uk>деньо́к</Uk>.
- **<Uk>-еньк-</Uk>** makes affectionate words: <Uk>ба́тенько</Uk>, <Uk>ні́женька</Uk>.

### Adjectives with -еньк-

The same affectionate suffix appears in adjectives: <Uk>мале́нький</Uk> (little, tiny) from <Uk>мали́й</Uk> (small). The dictionary defines it simply as "small in size" [src: goroh-dictionary]: <Uk>мале́нька дити́на</Uk> (a little child).

### A diminutive can become a new word

Some diminutives have their own meanings. <Uk>Ру́чка</Uk> is the diminutive of <Uk>рука́</Uk> (hand, arm), but it also means a handle and a pen [src: goroh-dictionary]. Check the dictionary when a word looks like a diminutive.

## Verb prefixes

A prefix added to a verb usually does two things: it makes the verb **perfective**, and it often adds a meaning. [Aspect](/grammar/aspect/) shows plain pairs such as <Uk>писа́ти</Uk> / <Uk>написа́ти</Uk>. Other prefixes change the meaning more.

  rows={[

    ['за-', 'записа́ти', 'to write down, to record'],
    ['пере-', 'переписа́ти', 'to rewrite, to copy out'],
    ['під-', 'підписа́ти', 'to sign'],
    ['ви-', 'ви́писати', 'to write out, to copy out (a passage)'],
    ['до-', 'дописа́ти', 'to finish writing'],
  ]}

/>

<GrammarTable
  caption="One verb, many prefixes: чита́ти (to read)"
  headers={['Prefix', 'Perfective verb', 'Meaning']}
  rows={[
    ['про-', 'прочита́ти', 'to read (through)'],
    ['пере-', 'перечита́ти', 'to reread; also to read through'],

A verb with a new meaning needs its own imperfective partner, usually made with a suffix: <Uk>перепи́сувати</Uk> (to be rewriting, to rewrite regularly), <Uk>підпи́сувати</Uk>, <Uk>перечи́тувати</Uk> [src: goroh-dictionary]. The prefixes on verbs of motion work in the same way, with mostly physical meanings: <Uk>при-</Uk> (arrive), <Uk>ви-</Uk> (go out), <Uk>за-</Uk> (drop in). See [Verbs of motion](/grammar/verbs-of-motion/).

### Spelling: з- or с-

The prefix <Uk>з-</Uk> becomes <Uk>с-</Uk> before <Uk>к</Uk>, <Uk>п</Uk>, <Uk>т</Uk>, <Uk>ф</Uk>, <Uk>х</Uk>: <Uk>сказа́ти</Uk>, <Uk>сфотографува́ти</Uk>. Before everything else it stays <Uk>з-</Uk>: <Uk>зроби́ти</Uk>. Before two consonants it is often <Uk>зі-</Uk>: <Uk>зігра́ти</Uk> [src: pravopys-2026, §31]. Other prefixes keep their final letter: <Uk>розкажи́</Uk>, <Uk>відпочи́ти</Uk>, not *<Uk>роскажи</Uk>* [src: pravopys-2026, §31].

## Noun suffixes

Many nouns for people, actions and qualities are made with a few suffixes. The orthography gives the spelling of each of them [src: pravopys-2026, §32].

<GrammarTable
  caption="Common noun suffixes"
  headers={['Suffix', 'Meaning', 'Examples']}
  rows={[
    ['-ник, -івник, -льник', 'a person who does something', 'працівни́к (worker), уболіва́льник (fan, supporter)'],
    ['-тель', 'a person who does something', 'вихова́тель (educator), вчи́тель (teacher)'],
    ['-ець', 'a person', 'мовозна́вець (linguist), перемо́жець (winner)'],
    ['-ість', 'a quality', 'ра́дість (joy), сві́жість (freshness)'],
    ['-ння, -іння, -ення', 'an action (from a verb)', 'чита́ння (reading), ході́ння (walking), зве́рнення (appeal)'],
    ['-еня', 'a young animal', 'кошеня́ (kitten), гусеня́ (gosling)'],
  ]}
  ukColumns={[2]}
/>

Two spelling points from the orthography: the suffixes <Uk>-ість</Uk>, <Uk>-тель</Uk>, <Uk>-ець</Uk> are written with a soft sign, and the action nouns are written with <Uk>-нн-</Uk> [src: pravopys-2026, §32].

## Feminine job titles

The orthography describes how nouns for women are made from masculine nouns, with the suffixes <Uk>-к-(а)</Uk>, <Uk>-иц-(я)</Uk>, <Uk>-ин-(я)</Uk> and <Uk>-ес-(а)</Uk> [src: pravopys-2026, §32]:

- **<Uk>-к-(а)</Uk>** is the most widely used, because it combines with many kinds of stems: <Uk>а́вторка</Uk>, <Uk>дизайнерка</Uk>, <Uk>дире́кторка</Uk>, <Uk>реда́кторка</Uk>, <Uk>студе́нтка</Uk>.
- **<Uk>-иц-(я)</Uk>** goes on stems in <Uk>-ник</Uk> and <Uk>-ень</Uk>: <Uk>пора́дниця</Uk> (adviser), <Uk>учени́ця</Uk> (pupil).
- **<Uk>-ин-(я)</Uk>** goes on stems in <Uk>-ець</Uk> and on some other stems: <Uk>продавчиня</Uk> (shop assistant), <Uk>кравчи́ня</Uk> (dressmaker), <Uk>майстри́ня</Uk>.
- **<Uk>-ес-(а)</Uk>** is rare: <Uk>поете́са</Uk>.

## Common mistakes and limits

<Note type="mistake">
  **Reading every diminutive as "small".** <Uk>Ма́мочка</Uk> is not a small mother, and <Uk>ру́чка</Uk> can be a pen or a handle. Diminutives often show affection, or they have become separate words.
</Note>

<Note type="mistake">
  **Taking a prefixed verb as the plain partner.** <Uk>Записа́ти</Uk> is "to write down", not just the perfective of <Uk>писа́ти</Uk>. For the plain pair, learn <Uk>писа́ти</Uk> / <Uk>написа́ти</Uk>. See [Aspect](/grammar/aspect/).
</Note>

<Note type="mistake">
  **<Uk>з-</Uk> before a voiceless consonant.** Write <Uk>сказа́ти</Uk>, not *<Uk>зказати</Uk>* [src: pravopys-2026, §31].
</Note>

<Note type="remember">
  Suffixes and prefixes are patterns, not rules that work for every word. You cannot add any suffix to any word. Check new words in a dictionary before you use them.
</Note>
